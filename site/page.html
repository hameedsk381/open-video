Frame-exact video engine

Videos you can write, review and reproduce.

MotionRender turns a script into a voiced, captioned MP4. The same script gives the same frames every time, and a design review stops text that is cut off, too small or hidden under the captions before anything renders. Nothing needs an AI key.

$ npx motionrender init my-video
View on GitHub

Needs Node 22+, FFmpeg and a Chromium-based browser. MIT licensed.

Every frame here came out of the engine.

Eleven stills from four explainers, each written as a script of scenes and spoken lines, and one source card from a test script, labelled as fictional. The layouts were chosen to fit what each idea needed.

The captions follow the real voice, word by word, and the segmented bar at the bottom shows where the viewer is. The data-centre figures come from the IEA and Berkeley Lab; the scripts are examples of what an agent can write, not a claim about the topic.

A script in. A checked video out.

You describe scenes and what is said over them, never coordinates or frame numbers. The engine places everything, records the voice and shows you one sheet of the whole video before it renders.

1 · Write

Scenes and spoken lines

{ "kind": "stat",
  "value": "415 TWh",
  "label": "used by data centres in 2024",
  "note": "IEA, Energy and AI, 2025",
  "say": "In 2024, data centres used about
          four hundred and fifteen terawatt-hours." }

Fifteen scene kinds, thirteen looks, and a freeform board for anything else.

2 · Make

One command

$ mr make explainer.json --final

Built: composition.json (6 scenes, 1080x1920, 6 spoken lines)
Review: 0 errors, 4 warnings, 0 notes.
Video: sky-paper.mp4 (53.0 s)

It validates, builds, records the voice, reviews the design and renders. An error stops the render.

Built to be trusted, not just fast.

Most of what makes a generated video good is what gets caught before you see it.

A review that says no

When we added captions, the review refused to render two of our own videos: a closing call to action sat under them, and a three-line title ran into its first bullet. Both are fixed in the engine now. It reports text cut off, too small to read on a phone, or under the platform's controls.

review: 1 error · not rendered

The same frames every time

Every frame is a pure function of time. We changed how frames travel to the browser, which made rendering about a third faster, and 180 frames before and after were bit-identical.

94 ms → 62 ms per frame · identical output

No AI key needed

The agent that writes the script is the model. The engine draws, voices, checks and renders.

Captions that follow the voice

Timed to the words the voice actually says, with the word being spoken highlighted.

Sourced numbers

mr facts looks up Wikipedia, World Bank and exchange-rate figures without a key, and a script carries its sources on screen.

For agents

Hand it to your agent.

  • An MCP server for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex and Cursor.
  • One command sets a folder up with AGENTS.md, the MCP config and the skills.
  • The agent validates, looks at frames, reads the review sheet, then renders.
# Claude Code
claude mcp add motionrender -- npx -y motionrender mcp

# or set up a whole folder
npx -y motionrender setup .

# then ask for the video you want
mr make explainer.json --final
